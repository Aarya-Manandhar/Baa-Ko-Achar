Our Heritage

A Legacy of Authentic Nepali Pickles

In the heart of Nepal, where culinary traditions run deep, our journey began with a simple family recipe passed down through generations. Baa Ko Achar (Grandmother's Pickle) represents more than just a condiment – it's a celebration of our rich cultural heritage and the art of traditional pickle making.

Our story began in a small kitchen, where our grandmother would carefully prepare pickles using recipes that had been perfected over decades. What started as a family tradition has now grown into a beloved brand, bringing the authentic taste of Nepal to homes worldwide.

Today, we continue to honor our heritage by maintaining the same traditional methods and recipes, while ensuring the highest standards of quality and consistency in every jar we produce.
