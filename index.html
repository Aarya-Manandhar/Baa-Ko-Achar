Achar

Achar is a traditional South Asian pickle, especially popular in Nepali cuisine. Made by preserving fruits or vegetables in mustard oil and a blend of aromatic spices, achar offers a burst of bold, tangy, and spicy flavors. It's more than just a side — achar adds depth, heat, and character to every meal, making it an essential part of Nepali dining culture.

For those who love authentic Nepali flavors, our pickles are crafted with the finest ingredients and traditional recipes, ensuring every bite is a burst of taste and heritage.
